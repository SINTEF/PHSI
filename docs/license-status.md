# License and reuse authorization

The `phsi` package is licensed under the [MIT license](../LICENSE), with copyright attributed to SINTEF and contributors.

On 28 October 2026, the project owner explicitly confirmed authority to approve use of all four underlying codebases and to publish `phsi` under the MIT license. This authorization covers the adapted material from pseudo-Hamiltonian system identification, pseudo-Hamiltonian neural networks, sparse identification of port-Hamiltonian systems and neural Hamiltonian ordinary differential equations. It resolves the permission questions recorded during the initial source inventory.

The two supplied pseudo-Hamiltonian neural-network MIT notices remain unchanged in `third_party/`: the root notice names SINTEF in 2025, and the nested notice retains its original bracketed 2022 SINTEF Digital wording. Upstream repositories themselves have not been modified. The sparse-identification code is still described as not yet public.

Scientific attribution and adaptations are documented in [attribution](attribution.md), and inspected source identities remain in `source-provenance.json`. Retain these notices and assess the permissions of any newly added source material separately.
